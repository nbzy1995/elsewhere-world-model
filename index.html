    Model activity
    
    
    A world-model experiment.
    The video comes from LingBot World 2 on Reactor. Your movement and descriptions steer what it generates.
    Use the game controls, choose an interaction, or describe your own action. Optional prompts can add objects or change the world.
    This is exploratory video, not a complete game simulation. Objects may change or disappear; place and date prompts are not verified geography, history, or forecasts. Driving and building are visual requests, not simulated mechanics.
    The Empire State future, paired camera views and lunar destinations use prepared scene references. Changing between them starts a new generated view; continuous flight and camera geometry are not simulated.
    The computer and robot workbench are working local apps. Notes, neural-network weights and robot assembly persist independently of the video. The workbench has exact assembly and joint controls, without a physics or electrical simulator.
    Sessions end after five minutes. “End session” releases the connection. Pausing retains it.
